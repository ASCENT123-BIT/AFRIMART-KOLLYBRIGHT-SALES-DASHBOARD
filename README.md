# AfriMart Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive Excel sales dashboard for **AfriMart**, a retailer selling everyday products across 10 African countries. It brings the raw sales data together in one view so management can track revenue, cost, profit and units sold, and compare performance by country, product and year.

**Business problem solved:** Sales data spread across rows and sheets makes it hard to see which markets and products drive profit. The dashboard gives decision-makers a quick, filterable view of where the business earns its money and where margins can improve.

## ❓ Business Questions Answered
- How much total revenue, cost and profit is the business generating?
- Which countries generate the most revenue and profit?
- How does the profit margin compare across countries?
- Which products contribute the most revenue and profit?
- How have revenue and profit changed from year to year?
- Which countries sell the most units?

## 📊 Key KPIs
- **Total Revenue:** 10,447,536,600
- **Total Cost of Units Sold:** 8,348,167,350
- **Total Profit:** 2,099,369,250
- **Overall Profit Margin:** about 20% (Profit ÷ Revenue)
- **Total Products Sold (units):** 1,017,885
- **Total Number of Products:** 7

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
- **PivotTables and PivotCharts** for aggregation and visuals
- **Slicers** (Date, Product, Country) for interactive filtering
- **Conditional formatting and dashboard layout** for presentation

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Healthy, consistent margins:** The business earns roughly 20% profit on revenue, and country profit margins sit in a tight band of about 19% to 22%, so no market is a major outlier.
- **Revenue is concentrated in a few products:** Rice (50kg bag) is the largest revenue contributor (more than half of the revenue pie), followed by Palm Oil (5L). Lower-priced items such as Detergent, Garri and Mobile Data Bundles contribute much smaller shares.
- **Profit follows revenue by product:** The Profit by Product chart rises steeply toward the highest-value product, showing that a small number of products drive most of the profit.
- **Revenue and profit are stable over time:** The yearly chart shows revenue and profit staying at similar levels across years, with profit consistently a small fraction of revenue.
- **Volume varies across countries:** Units sold differ by market, with Nigeria and Côte d'Ivoire among the highest-volume countries in the chart, and Ghana and Senegal at the lower end of the labeled countries.
- **Interactive analysis:** Slicers let users drill into a single year (the dashboard is currently filtered to 2024), product or country.

## ✅ Conclusion
This project shows how raw sales data can be turned into a clear, interactive dashboard using Excel PivotTables and slicers. It highlights AfriMart's roughly 20% profit margin, its dependence on a few high-value products, and how performance compares across countries, helping the business focus on its strongest products and markets and look for ways to lift margins.
