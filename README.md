# Retail_Sales_Analysis-
nteractive Excel sales dashboard for AfriMart, tracking 10.4B+ in total revenue and 1M+ units sold across 10 African countries and 7 products. Built using PivotTables, PivotCharts and slicers to analyze revenue, cost, profit and margin by country, product and year, showing a steady ~20% margin driven by high-value products like rice and palm oil.
